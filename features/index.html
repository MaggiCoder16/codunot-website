⚡ Command-Based Features

Explicit commands for specific functionality

🎭 Mode & Personality Commands

  • /funmode - Casual, lighthearted responses with emojis and humor
  • /seriousmode - Professional, structured, fact-based responses
  • /roastmode - Humorous, lighthearted roasts and comebacks
  • /teachmerizz online - Dating/texting coach for online conversations
  • /teachmerizz irl - Real-life social coaching and confidence tips
  • /chessmode - Play chess against Codunot AI (full engine with legal move validation, SAN/UCI support, castling, promotions, en passant, resign detection, and AI hints during games)

All Free

🎨 Creative Generation (Vote Required)

  • /generate_image [prompt] - AI-generated images with enhanced prompts
  • /generate_video [prompt] - Text-to-video generation (~1 min render time)
  • /generate_tts [text] [language] [voice] - Text-to-speech with 300+ voices across 60+ languages. Expanded with Microsoft Edge TTS and readable picker names like English, Hindi, Arabic

Vote Required

🎵 Music & Audio (Vote Required)

  • /play [song/URL] - Play music with audio filters (Normal, Bass Boost, Nightcore, Slowed, 8D, Treble, Lo-Fi, Vaporwave)
  • Supports YouTube, SoundCloud, and Spotify (songs, playlists, and albums via Lavalink)
  • /queue - View current music queue with position tracking
  • /volume_up and /volume_down - Fine-tune playback volume quickly
  • /autoplay - Toggle whether playback continues after queue end
  • /lyrics - Fetch full lyrics for the currently playing track
  • Music controls: Previous, Pause/Resume, Skip/Next, Stop, and inline 🔉/🔊 buttons on player UI
  • Auto-disconnect after 10 minutes of inactivity

Vote Required

🛡️ Moderation & Safety

  • /setup-moderation - 6-step moderation wizard (AutoMod, logs, roles, links, anti-spam, anti-raid)
  • /automod - Toggle AutoMod on/off post-setup
  • /warn, /warns, /clearwarns, /case - Warning and case-management workflow
  • /ban, /unban, /modkick, /mute, /unmute - Core staff moderation actions
  • /clear, /slowmode, /lock, /unlock, /userinfo - Channel and moderation utilities
  • Natural Language Moderation: Mention the bot with plain English, e.g. @Codunot timeout @user 10m for spamming, @Codunot ban @user for raiding, @Codunot clear 20

Core moderation: Free Advanced: Premium / Gold (/tempban, /massban, /modstats, /note, + more coming for Premium/Gold)
