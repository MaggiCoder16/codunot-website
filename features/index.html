⚡ Command-Based Features

Explicit commands for specific functionality

🎭 Mode & Personality Commands

  • /funmode - Casual, lighthearted responses with emojis and humor
  • /seriousmode - Professional, structured, fact-based responses
  • /roastmode - Humorous, lighthearted roasts and comebacks
  • /teachmerizz online - Dating/texting coach for online conversations
  • /teachmerizz irl - Real-life social coaching and confidence tips
  • /chessmode - Play chess against Codunot AI

All Free

🎨 Creative Generation (Vote Required)

  • /generate_image [prompt] - AI-generated images with enhanced prompts
  • /generate_video [prompt] - Text-to-video generation (~1 min render time)
  • /generate_tts [text] [language] [voice] - Text-to-speech with 20+ voices, 8 languages (min 20 chars)

Vote Required

🎵 Music & Audio (Vote Required)

  • /play [song/URL] - Play music with audio filters (bass boost, nightcore, slowed, 8D, lofi, etc.)
  • /queue - View current music queue
  • Music controls: Pause/Resume, Skip, Previous, Stop, Volume buttons

Vote Required

🛡️ Moderation & Safety

  • /setup-moderation - 6-step moderation wizard (AutoMod, logs, roles, links, anti-spam, anti-raid)
  • /automod - Toggle AutoMod on/off post-setup
  • /warn, /warns, /clearwarns, /case - Warning and case-management workflow
  • /ban, /unban, /modkick, /mute, /unmute - Core staff moderation actions
  • /clear, /slowmode, /lock, /unlock, /userinfo - Channel and moderation utilities

Core moderation: Free Advanced: Premium / Gold (/tempban, /massban, /modstats, /note)

🔧 Developer & Utility Tools

  • /test_code [code] - Validate code, get summaries, auto-fix errors
  • /browse [URL] - Explicitly fetch and analyze webpage content
  • /google_maps [location] [query] - Search for places with ratings, reviews, hours, and directions
  • /transcribe [video_url] - Transcribe YouTube/Twitch/X/Kick videos (max 30 mins)
  • /guessage @user - Estimate user's age from message patterns

/test_code, /browse, /google_maps = Free
