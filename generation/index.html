Everything Included In Generation + Music

Generation features

  • /generate_image for original prompt-to-image outputs.
  • /edit_image for prompt-driven edits to uploaded images.
  • /analyse_image for image understanding and visual breakdowns.
  • /merge_image to combine 2-4 images into one result.
  • /generate_video for short cinematic prompt-based clips.
  • /generate_tts for text-to-speech with 300+ voices and 60+ languages.
  • /transcribe to convert supported videos into readable text.

Music playback features

  • /play, /queue, /skip, /stop, /nowplaying, and /history for full queue control.
  • /shuffle, /loop, /remove, /volume, and /autoplay for session management.
  • /lyrics for live lyric lookup when available.
  • Audio filters: Normal, Bass Boost, Nightcore, Slowed, 8D, Treble, Lo-Fi, and Vaporwave.
  • Playlist tools: /playlistcreate and /playlist for saving, loading, and managing playlists.

Music bitrate by tier: Basic: 64 kbps · Premium: 90 kbps · Gold: 160 kbps · Enterprise: 320 kbps.
