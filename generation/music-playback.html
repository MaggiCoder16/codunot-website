Music Playback

Join a voice channel and use /play for queue-based playback, filters, inline controls, and supported links from YouTube, SoundCloud, and Spotify. Playlists, loop modes, smarter autoplay, and persistence keep sessions flowing. Vote Required

Playback, Queue, And Filters

Use /play [song name or URL] to start playback. If something is already playing, the next song is queued automatically.

Audio filters include Normal, Bass Boost, Nightcore, Slowed, 8D, Treble, Lo-Fi, and Vaporwave. The bot disconnects after 10 minutes of inactivity.

Extra controls include /queue, /volume_up, /volume_down, /autoplay, and /lyrics.

Playlists And Loop Modes

Create playlists in one step with /playlistcreate, then browse and manage them using /playlist.

Loop control is simple with /loop off, /loop song, and /loop queue to match your listening style.

The playlist browser uses clean, modern embeds inspired by Hydra and FredBoat.

Autoplay And Persistence

Next tracks load faster, typically in about 1-3 seconds, for smoother playback.

Duplicate prevention avoids playing songs similar to the last five tracks.

Queues, playlists, and history stay saved across bot restarts.

Complete Music Playback Feature List

  • Voice playback entry: /play supports direct links and search terms.
  • Queue controls: /queue, /skip, /stop, /nowplaying, /remove, and /history.
  • Session controls: /shuffle, /loop (off/song/queue), /autoplay, and /volume.
  • Music extras: /lyrics, duplicate prevention, and saved queue/history persistence.
  • Filters: Normal, Bass Boost, Nightcore, Slowed, 8D, Treble, Lo-Fi, and Vaporwave.
  • Playlist commands: /playlistcreate and /playlist for create/add/delete/view flows.

Music bitrate by tier: Basic: 64 kbps · Premium: 90 kbps · Gold: 160 kbps · Enterprise: 320 kbps.
