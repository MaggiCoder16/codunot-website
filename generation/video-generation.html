Video Generation

Generate short cinematic clips from text prompts for previews, visual concepts, and motion moodboards. Vote Required

Prompt To Motion

Use /generate_video [prompt] [model] for short text-to-video clips. This works well for atmosphere tests, short visual loops, and concept shots.

Render time is roughly one minute, depending on load and prompt complexity.

Audio is included in the player preview.

Available models by tier

  • Basic: codu-vidgen-basic
  • Premium: codu-vidgen-basic, codu-vidgen-pro
  • Gold: codu-vidgen-basic, codu-vidgen-pro, codu-vidgen-strongest
  • Enterprise: codu-vidgen-basic, codu-vidgen-pro, codu-vidgen-strongest
