Music Playback — /play

Join a voice channel and type /play [song name or URL] to start listening. Supports YouTube, SoundCloud, and Spotify songs and playlists via Lavalink.

If something is already playing, your song gets added to the queue and auto-plays when the current track finishes. Paste a playlist URL and the entire list loads — first track plays immediately, rest get queued.

Audio filters: Normal, Bass Boost, Nightcore, Slowed, 8D, Treble, Lo-Fi, and Vaporwave. HD audio for free users, 320kbps for Premium/Gold. Bot auto-disconnects after 10 minutes of inactivity.
