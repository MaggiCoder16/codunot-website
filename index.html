Music Playback — /play

Join a voice channel and type /play [song name or URL] to start listening. Supports YouTube and SoundCloud songs and playlists.

If something is already playing, your song gets added to the queue and auto-plays when the current track finishes. Paste a playlist URL and the entire list loads — first track plays immediately, rest get queued.

HD audio for free users, 320kbps for Premium/Gold. Bot auto-disconnects after 10 minutes of inactivity.
