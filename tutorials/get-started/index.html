Getting Started

How to start using Codunot in your Discord server

Last update: April 24, 2026

Step 1: Invite and approve permissions

Open the invite link, select your server, and approve permissions for moderation, embeds, and command use. In under a minute, Codunot is ready for slash commands and mention-based chat.

Step 2: Choose where Codunot can reply

Use /configure server when you want replies anywhere, or use /configure channels to limit responses to 1-5 channels for cleaner organization.

Step 3: Choose your style and speed

Set behavior with /funmode, /seriousmode, or /roastmode, then run /model to choose the model that fits your quality/speed preference in each channel.

Frequently asked questions

Is Premium required to start? No. The free tier is enough to launch core chat, moderation, and starter workflows.

Can different channels use different models? Yes. Model selection is channel/DM scoped, so teams can optimize by workflow.
