Growth Planning

How to choose the right Codunot tier for your server

Last update: April 24, 2026

Step 1: Measure actual command usage

Track daily AI chats, attachments, and music hours before upgrading so tier decisions are based on real demand.

Step 2: Match your bottleneck to tier features

If media quotas cap your workflows, compare Premium vs Gold. If moderation scale is the blocker, evaluate Gold or Enterprise features first.

Step 3: Upgrade at workflow pain points

Upgrade when daily limits interrupt service, not just because of potential growth. This keeps spend aligned with measurable value.

Step 4: Re-evaluate every month

As events, member count, and support load change, re-check tier fit monthly and adjust before seasonal spikes.
